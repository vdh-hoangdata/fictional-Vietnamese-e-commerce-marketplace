# VietCommerce

VietCommerce sells electronics, fashion, beauty, home appliances, groceries and
lifestyle goods through independent and company-operated sellers. Warehouses
hold seller-owned inventory. Web and mobile shoppers can pay by COD, transfer,
card or wallet. Split shipments follow seller/warehouse boundaries.

The generator models two years of demand, uneven customer/product popularity,
campaign exposure, price changes, fulfillment, returns and service contacts.
Source extracts contain operational defects layered over the coherent reference.
Association between marketing and purchases is observable; causal attribution is
not known. All contacts use reserved `.invalid` domains and deliberately invalid
phone tokens. Street lines are explicitly fictional. Real region names provide
coarse context only; no real district, postal or household precision is claimed.

The snapshot cutoff is 2025-12-31 23:59:59 Vietnam time. Orders near the cutoff
can remain in progress. Settlements, returns and service work after the cutoff
are absent, providing a realistic right-censored observation window.
